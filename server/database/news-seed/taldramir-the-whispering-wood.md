Blizzard has introduced **Taldramir, the Whispering Wood**, as World of Warcraft: Forever’s first legendary, in a “Forging Forever” feature and the WoW Weekly recap that followed launch. Official copy describes the weapon as forged by Wētā Workshop and wielded by adventurers in Forever.

That is as far as the official posts go. Blizzard has not published stats, effects, class or specialization restrictions, or a method for obtaining the weapon. This note does not fill those gaps from datamining or comment speculation.

Wowhead’s report, cited as a secondary source, identifies the reveal model as a two-handed staff with a clear Druid theme and notes that the weapon also appears in Skyborne key art. Treat those observations as Wowhead’s, not as official item tooltip data.

## What this means for your UI / MagguuUI

Until Blizzard publishes how the legendary is obtained and what it does, there is nothing for a MagguuUI pack to surface beyond ordinary item tooltips once the weapon exists on a character. Do not expect an addon to track an unpublished acquisition path. When official acquisition notes appear, they will belong in a later article, not in a guessed tracker.
