November’s Trading Post in modern World of Warcraft is built around a **Sweet Tooth** theme. In an official WoW Weekly recap, Blizzard said this month is about “indulging your sweet side” and that the post is “serving up a sugar rush” of new collection items. The only reward named in that announcement is the monthly Traveler’s Log bonus: the **Hot Motocross Bunmover 4600** mount.

Blizzard’s flavor text for the mount is: “When dessert manufacturing meets speed optimization. Intact bun delivery not guaranteed.”

## How the official bonus works

The Bunmover 4600 is not described as a Trader’s Tender purchase. You earn it by completing Traveler’s Log activities and filling the monthly log. The Traveler’s Log lives in the Adventure Guide (the default shortcut is Shift-J). The same weekly post tells players to complete activities to earn Trader’s Tender and to fill the log to claim the bonus mount.

Blizzard did not publish a full November catalog, individual Tender prices, or an exact end timestamp in that announcement. The in-game Trading Post is the place to see this month’s shop list and any countdown on the log. Unofficial item lists circulated after the preview; those prices and names are left out here because they were not in the official post.

## Where to find the Trading Post

The official weekly names four locations:

- **Stormwind:** T&W (Tawney and Wilder), just outside the Mage District
- **Orgrimmar:** the Zen’shiri Trading Post, next to Grommash Hold
- **Dornogal:** the Foregrounds, on the Isle of Dorn, where representatives of both shops are open
- **Renewed Silvermoon City:** representatives of both Tawney and Wilder and the Zen’shiri Trading Post

New and returning Midnight players can also take Blizzard’s in-game Trading Post tour, which covers where the post is and how additional Trader’s Tender is earned through the Traveler’s Log.

The same weekly also promoted other November collectibles, including the Living Heartsbane Cauldron mount and the Grimtide Estate and Grimtide Decor housing packs. Those were not listed as Trading Post items in the announcement, so they should be checked in the shop or housing interface rather than assumed to be part of the Sweet Tooth catalog.

## What this means for your UI / MagguuUI

The Trading Post, Traveler’s Log, and Collector’s Cache are first-party Midnight windows. MagguuUI does not grant Tender or the Bunmover mount, and you do not need an addon to claim the log reward. If a custom layout hides the Adventure Guide or vendor interaction, restore or keybind Shift-J and make sure the Stormwind, Orgrimmar, Dornogal, or Silvermoon vendors remain clickable so you can finish the November log before the month turns over.
