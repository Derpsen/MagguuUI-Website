Talebound, Midnight’s new PvE action RPG mode, arrives on December 1, 2026, with regional maintenance, alongside Echoes of the Worldcore. You play as part of a mysterious Storyteller’s macabre chronicles, solo or with friends, through themed tales on Normal, Veteran, and Heroic difficulties.

## From Red Hollow to Quillgrave

The official overview starts the journey in Red Hollow, on a darkened road that leads to the Storyteller and then to Quillgrave, the lantern-lit village used as a hub between runs. After each tale, you return to work with four vendors: the Arcanist (potions, draughts, and flasks found in the tale), the Scribe (talents and abilities), the Antiquarian (passives that change how a tale can be approached), and the Outfitter (starting health, damage, energy, and focus).

Four stories are listed, each with its own setting and bosses:

- **Darkhaven**, home to Konrad von Sonnesheim, the Lord Marshal, and Alexandre Dozsenc, the Blood Bishop, after a plague has isolated the town
- **Greenthistle Wood**, around the villages of Shady Hollow and Riverton, with two witches, a giant, and other fiends
- **Greywake’s Expedition**, following losses to Zzyg’th, the Hadal Tyrant, and Chathoa, the Herald from Beyond
- **Grove of the Night Court**, a chronicle of Tilywth, the Fairy Queen, and Maernok, the Nightmare King

Collections of these stories are also available in town if you want to hear them again.

## Classes, movement, and rewards

Every class can double jump, dodge, Evade (a brief window of immunity), Sprint, and Guard. You choose one of three archetypes, each with a talent tree of 27 points. Tales of Valor spent at the Scribe unlock extra abilities and later upgrade those talents.

- **Witch** leans on lightning, poison, spiders, moonlight, thorns, and ravens.
- **Slayer** mixes a sword with a gun and monster-hunter tools.
- **Inquisitor** uses holy and fire attacks, including several spells that can be used while moving.

Progression currency includes Ducats, which convert into the resources used to advance in Talebound, and Bound Silver, spent on transmog sets, individual pieces, and other cosmetics in the Talebound store. That store is available from the character selection screen, the Game Menu, or Nib in Quillgrave. Official reward art shown with the announcement includes the Witch Hunter’s Shadow Attire set, a Shadowbound Storybook mount, and Quillgrave housing decor.

To start, open Talebound from the World of Warcraft menu, create a hero, and choose an archetype. After the Red Hollow introduction and a first look at Quillgrave, you pick a story and a difficulty.

## What this means for your UI / MagguuUI

Talebound is a first-party game mode with its own vendors, talent panel, and store. MagguuUI will not replace those windows or grant Bound Silver. When the mode goes live on December 1, use the official menu entry to start it, and keep the in-mode UI visible rather than expecting a Retail layout to cover Quillgrave or the Talebound store.
