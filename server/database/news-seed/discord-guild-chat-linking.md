Blizzard has added official Discord account linking and guild chat mirroring to both modern World of Warcraft and World of Warcraft: Forever. Messages can travel between a linked Discord channel and in-game guild chat, so raid calls, PvP recruiting, and casual guild talk can continue when some members are logged out.

The feature is opt-in. Nothing is mirrored until a player connects a Discord account, and a guild channel is not linked until a guild leader or an officer with the right permissions sets it up.

## Link your own account first

In game, open the Game Menu (Escape), go to Options, and open the Social tab. A new control, Enable Discord Functionality, starts the connection. You will leave the client briefly to confirm the link, so you should already be signed in to Discord.

If a player has not linked an account, their in-game messages do not appear in the Discord channel. Linked messages are visually distinct from ordinary guild chat, so you can see which side a line came from and put Discord traffic on the chat tabs you already use.

You can also choose the name that appears when a Discord message shows up in game:

- **Default**, which currently uses the last online character
- **Last Online Character Name**, using the last character on your Battle.net account that is in that guild
- **Discord Global Name**, using your Discord global name

## What guild leaders need to do

On the Discord side, the target channel must allow the people who will complete the link to Manage Channels, View Channel, and Send Messages.

In World of Warcraft, a guild leader or an eligible officer then:

1. Opens the Guild & Communities UI (default hotkey J).
2. Selects the Roster tab.
3. Opens Guild Settings.
4. Chooses Discord Settings under the Guild Control dropdown.
5. Picks a Discord server they belong to, selects a linkable channel, and confirms the link.

From there, guild leaders can keep Discord traffic in regular `/guild` chat or send it to a dedicated `/discord` channel. You can also create a separate Discord chat as its own channel inside the game.

Blizzard highlighted the same feature in this week’s WoW Weekly recap, alongside Forever’s launch and first-party gamepad support. Setup is identical in tone on Midnight and Forever, even though the two games use different default interfaces.

## What this means for your UI / MagguuUI

This is a first-party Blizzard feature. MagguuUI does not need to provide the link, and you should not look for a third-party Discord bridge if the official one already covers your guild. After you enable it, check your chat tabs so mirrored lines are visible and not hidden by a filter. If your guild leader routes traffic into a dedicated `/discord` channel, add that channel to a tab you actually watch. Custom chat layouts in MagguuUI or other addons should pick up the new channel the same way they pick up any other official chat type; no extra addon update is required just to turn the feature on.
