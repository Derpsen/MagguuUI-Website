World of Warcraft’s 22nd Anniversary celebration runs from November 17 to December 8, 2026, in the Caverns of Time in Tanaris. Holiday Celebrants offer a quest, and portals in Orgrimmar and Stormwind provide a ride to the event grounds.

Players receive an automatic 10% experience and reputation buff during the event. Completing the repeatable “Celebrate Good Fun!” quest from Yllana in Tanaris each day adds another 10%. The anniversary buff also stacks with other experience and reputation increases. The official post lists a 25% experience buff from the Five Warband Mentors: Midnight achievement, a 15% experience and gold bonus from War Mode, and a 10% bonus from the Draught of Ten Lands potion for characters level 49 and lower. Together, the two anniversary bonuses are a 20% experience and reputation increase from the event itself.

## Currency, vendors, and the Loyal Watchman set

Activities award currencies that can be spent on the grounds, including Timewarped Badges from Timewalking events, Marks of Honor exchanged for legacy weapons and armor at PvP vendors, Ancient Mana, and Polished Pet Charms. Officially named vendors include:

- **Traeya**, selling Tier 2 armor for Timewarped Badges
- **Time-Displaced Thanthaldis Snowgleam** and **Time-Displaced Jorek Ironside**, selling scaling PvP-themed weapons and armor for Marks of Honor and Timewarped Badges
- **Historian Ma’di**, the anniversary rewards vendor, for Timewarped Badges
- **Notary Grably**, for Bronze Celebration titles

The official reward example is the Loyal Watchman ensemble: helm, gauntlets, trousers, cuirass, spaulders, belt, and boots.

Quests and activity completions are tracked Warband-wide. They can be finished on any character in the Warband and count toward the weekly amount of Warband Transferable Tokens the Warband can earn. There is no requirement to run the event on multiple characters each week unless you want to.

## What this means for your UI / MagguuUI

The anniversary grounds, vendors, and daily quest are first-party event UI. MagguuUI does not need a special module for the buffs or the Loyal Watchman set to be available. If a custom layout hides holiday NPCs, the Tanaris portals, or the vendor windows, restore those interactions before December 8 so daily “Celebrate Good Fun!” and the weekly token cap remain reachable.
