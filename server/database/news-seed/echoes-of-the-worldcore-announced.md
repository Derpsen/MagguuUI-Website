Blizzard has officially announced the next Midnight content update, **Echoes of the Worldcore**, and confirmed it is currently on the Public Test Realm. No live release date has been published. A later update, **Eclipse**, is also officially announced as Midnight’s final major content update, again with no calendar date. Everything below is announced or on the PTR, not live in Season 2 unless noted.

## Echoes of the Worldcore (on the PTR)

The official overview sends players back to Dornogal. Xal’atath has breached the Worldcore, and the damaged Coreway is the way through. The new campaign, “The Return to Dornogal,” has players helping Arator, Brinthe, Dagran, and Brann find a path forward.

**Talebound** arrives with this update as a new PvE action RPG mode. You are pulled into the tales of a mysterious Storyteller, solo or with friends, through enemies, bosses, puzzles, treasure, and relics. Completing quests, achievements, and Tales of the Storyteller earns Bound Silver, a Warband-bound currency spent on cosmetics in the Talebound store from character selection, the Game Menu, or Nib in Quillgrave. Ducats convert into the resources used to progress. A Talebound demo is already on the PTR, with no subscription required.

**Lorewalking** adds four topics for level 10 and above, started with Apprentice Lorewalker Li Li Stormstout in Stormwind, Orgrimmar, or Silvermoon City: Jaina, Thrall, the Titans, and Tyr. You can ask questions in the Lorewalking UI, leave or pause a story, and repeat it. Starting a different story before you finish one resets progress, and other quests cannot be progressed while Lorewalking is active.

**Turbulent Timeways** starts when Echoes of the Worldcore launches and lasts eight weeks. The Markeet of the Infinite skyriding mount comes from “Master of the Turbulent Timeways VI,” earned by maintaining “Mastery of the Timeways” for any four weeks. A Timewalking dungeon grants Knowledge of the Timeways, a 5% stacking buff; after four dungeons it becomes Mastery of Timeways, granting 30% experience for the buff’s duration. Timewalking vendors receive new housing decor, and Aethas Sunreaver’s Timewalking quests in Silvermoon City offer increased rewards for a limited time, including a Heroic-level Cache of Quel’Thalas Treasures.

The Omnium Folio also expands, with two additional Motes of Omnial Inquiry and two new runes: Empowerment and Critical Mass.

## Later, still announced: Eclipse and Season 3

The BlizzCon 2026 What’s Next panel described Eclipse as the final major Midnight update, a confrontation with Xal’atath and the Devouring Host. Announced features include a new zone, the Mantle Vault, and new allies, the Thraegar; a three-boss dungeon, Thraegar’s Stand, that will enter the Season 3 Mythic+ pool; an eight-boss raid, the Worldcore, source of Midnight’s Edge, the first Legendary of Midnight and of the Worldsoul Saga (a caster dagger; defeating the raid is only one unlock step); and a Lair boss, Noctinaxx, in the Gearhold.

Season 3 begins shortly after Eclipse launches. Its announced Mythic+ pool is Thraegar’s Stand, Magisters’ Terrace, Maisara Cavern, The Underrot, Iron Docks, Sanguine Depths, The Stonecore, and Uldaman: Legacy of Tyr. Top 1% and a new top 5% reward will be judged by specialization, not overall rating. Forsaken, Night Elf, and Troll characters will be able to become Paladins. No ship date has been published.

## What this means for your UI / MagguuUI

Echoes of the Worldcore is still on the PTR. Do not treat PTR layouts, Talebound windows, or Lorewalking UI as live MagguuUI requirements. When the update reaches live realms, Talebound’s store is a first-party panel (character select, Game Menu, or Nib in Quillgrave) and does not need an addon. MagguuUI will not replace Talebound’s separate game mode.
