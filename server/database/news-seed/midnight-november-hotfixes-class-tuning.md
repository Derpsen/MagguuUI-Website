Blizzard’s live hotfix list for modern World of Warcraft, last headed November 6, 2026, covers a late-October class pass and a further easing of The Venomous Abyss. The notes apply to Midnight alongside other versions of the game. Some changes go live immediately; others wait for a realm restart.

## October 27 class tuning

The October 27 entry is the most recent PvE class pass in that list.

- **Feral Druid:** all damage increased by 5%. This does not affect PvP.
- **Restoration Druid:** Verdancy healing increased by 60%, Regrowth healing increased by 10%, and Improved Swiftmend now increases Swiftmend healing by 40%, up from 30%. These also do not affect PvP. Developers said the goal was to help Restoration handle burst damage in dungeons, where it was behind other healers, while leaving raid performance alone.
- **Marksmanship Hunter:** Trick Shots now increases the ricochet damage of Aimed Shot and Rapid Fire by 85%, up from 75%. Explosive Shot and Volley damage each increased by 10%, to raise the spec’s area damage.
- **Fire Mage:** all damage increased by 5% outside PvP.
- **Affliction Warlock:** Unstable Affliction damage increased by 20% outside PvP.
- **Destruction Warlock:** Rain of Fire damage increased by 20%.
- **Subtlety Rogue:** all ability damage reduced by 8%. Trickster’s Fazed now increases damage by 5%, down from 7%. Developers said a compensation buff tied to patch 12.1.5 overshot, and that those patch changes had helped Trickster more than Deathstalker.

Mistweaver Monk received a fix so the Mistline talent more reliably heals the lowest-health target. A day earlier, Enhancement Shaman’s Voltaic Blaze was fixed when Ula’tek is the main target, and Affliction’s Cooldown Manager display for Summon Darkglare was corrected.

The same October 27 list also retunes several specs in PvP only, including a 5% Fire Mage damage reduction, Holy Paladin and Holy Priest healing increases, and buffs to Marksmanship and Dark Ranger, Devourer Demon Hunter, Frostfire Mage, and Restoration Shaman totems. Those PvP numbers do not change the PvE figures above.

## November 5–6 raid easing

On November 5 and 6, Blizzard reduced damage and health across several Venomous Abyss encounters. On Mythic, Vashnik’s Malignance damage was cut by 37%, and its damage-over-time duration is now 16 seconds. Ula’tek lost 15% health on Heroic. Health scaling for Ula’tek and several adds was adjusted on Raid Finder, Normal, and Heroic so smaller raids are accounted for, and base health was lowered so larger groups do not push those values up.

Ability damage was cut on all or most difficulties, including Unchecked Rage (61% on all difficulties), Caustic Waves, Malice, Virulent Spit, Falling Debris, and Viscous Echoes. Call of the Serpent summons fewer eggs on Normal and Heroic, Serpent’s Bite and Grasping Fangs hit fewer targets, and Phase 2 on Heroic now has eight eggs per side. Ula’tek no longer creates extra Caustic Waves from Spectral Tails during the Phase 3 wave walls on Heroic. Earlier bosses, including the Entombed Sentinels, Twin Fangs, and the Coiled Altar, were also tuned down, especially on Mythic.

October 27 also fixed Labyrinth scenarios that were not granting Great Vault credit.

## What this means for your UI / MagguuUI

These are combat and encounter changes, not a new interface patch. MagguuUI does not need an update for the damage and healing adjustments to apply. Review rotation notes for the buffed and nerfed specs, and note that several of the October 27 PvE changes explicitly do not affect PvP. The official Cooldown Manager fix for Summon Darkglare is already in the default UI.
