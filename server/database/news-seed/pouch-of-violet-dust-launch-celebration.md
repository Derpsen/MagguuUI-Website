The launch-week Twitch Drop for World of Warcraft: Forever is almost over. From November 4 at 4:30 p.m. PST to November 12 at 4:30 p.m. PST (1:30 a.m. on November 13 in Europe/Berlin), watching eligible World of Warcraft streams can unlock the Pouch of Violet Dust, a temporary campfire toy.

That watch window closes tomorrow afternoon in North America, or just after midnight in Central Europe. If you want the toy, finish the watch time and claim the drop before the cutoff.

## What the toy does

The Pouch of Violet Dust is available only in WoW: Forever. It turns a campfire purple and extends all camp benefits by one hour, for a total duration of two hours. Once it is used, anyone resting at that camp receives the same longer duration.

The toy can be used only during the “WoW: Forever Launch Celebration.” Blizzard says that event period expires in early December. After that, the toy is removed from collections and is no longer available in game. The watch window ending on November 12 and the later celebration expiry are separate dates; you need to claim the drop by November 12, and you can use the toy until the celebration ends in early December.

The effect sits on top of Forever’s camping system. A Basic Campfire can become a shared campsite, nearby players can add up to three profession-crafted Camp Features (one each), and sitting or crafting there for at least one minute grants those benefits. The camp buffs are mutually exclusive with the corresponding class buffs.

## How to earn it

Link your Twitch and Battle.net accounts at [account.battle.net/connections](https://account.battle.net/connections) before you start. If you have changed a password or the link, you may need to connect the accounts again; there is a seven-day cooldown before you can attach a different Twitch account.

Watch four hours of eligible World of Warcraft content on Twitch while Drops are active. Progress carries from channel to channel in the World of Warcraft category; watching more than one channel at once does not speed it up. You must claim the drop on the channel or in Twitch’s Drops Inventory. After you claim it, log into the Battle.net region where you want the item delivered first — the first region you enter is the one that receives it — and allow up to 24 hours for it to arrive. Unclaimed rewards expire seven days after they are claimed if no Battle.net account is linked.

Eligible Twitch regions include North America, Latin America, Europe, the Middle East, Africa, Japan, Taiwan, Southeast Asia, Australia, and New Zealand. Console, smart TV, and other TV apps do not support drops; use a PC or Mac browser or the Twitch app on Android or iOS.

A YouTube path exists as well: link Battle.net through YouTube Connected Apps and watch the official World of Warcraft channel for the full time. Those rewards can arrive in game automatically if the accounts are already connected.

## What this means for your UI / MagguuUI

The pouch is a first-party Forever toy, not an addon item, and MagguuUI does not need to display or grant it. After it arrives, use it at a campfire the same way you would use any other toy. If your Forever layout hides the toy box, bags, or camp interaction prompts, make sure those remain reachable until the celebration ends in early December. Camp benefits and class buffs do not stack when they correspond to one another, which is a game rule rather than a UI setting.
