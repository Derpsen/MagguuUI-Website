The launch-week Twitch Drop for World of Warcraft: Forever is over. From November 4 at 4:30 p.m. PST to November 12 at 4:30 p.m. PST (1:30 a.m. on November 13 in Europe/Berlin), watching eligible World of Warcraft streams could unlock the Pouch of Violet Dust, a temporary campfire toy. That watch window is closed, and this seed stays unpublished so the site does not present an ended drop as current.

## What the toy does

The Pouch of Violet Dust is available only in WoW: Forever. It turns a campfire purple and extends all camp benefits by one hour, for a total duration of two hours. Once it is used, anyone resting at that camp receives the same longer duration.

The toy can be used only during the “WoW: Forever Launch Celebration.” Blizzard says that event period expires in early December. After that, the toy is removed from collections and is no longer available in game. The watch window that ended on November 12 and the later celebration expiry are separate dates. New drops can no longer be earned; players who already claimed the toy can still use it until the celebration ends.

The effect sits on top of Forever’s camping system. A Basic Campfire can become a shared campsite, nearby players can add up to three profession-crafted Camp Features (one each), and sitting or crafting there for at least one minute grants those benefits. The camp buffs are mutually exclusive with the corresponding class buffs.

## How it was earned

The official drop required a linked Twitch and Battle.net account, four hours of eligible World of Warcraft content on Twitch while Drops were active, and a claim in Twitch’s Drops Inventory. Progress could move from channel to channel in the World of Warcraft category; watching more than one channel at once did not speed it up. After a claim, the first Battle.net region entered received the item, with up to 24 hours for delivery. Unclaimed rewards expired seven days after they were claimed if no Battle.net account was linked.

A YouTube path was also documented: link Battle.net through YouTube Connected Apps and watch the official World of Warcraft channel for the full time.

## What this means for your UI / MagguuUI

The pouch is a first-party Forever toy, not an addon item, and MagguuUI does not need to display or grant it. If you already have it, use it at a campfire the same way you would use any other toy until the celebration ends in early December. Camp benefits and class buffs do not stack when they correspond to one another, which is a game rule rather than a UI setting.
