Midnight Season 2’s Mythic+ rotation is the current dungeon pool in modern World of Warcraft. Blizzard opened the eight-dungeon lineup on Heroic and Mythic 0 in the week of August 11, 2026, and started Mythic+ Season 2 in the week of August 18. Mythic 0 used a weekly lockout and dropped item level 292 gear in that first week, then moved to a daily reset when the season began, still at item level 292.

## The official eight-dungeon pool

The Season 2 launch post and the Curse of Ula’tek notes list the same rotation:

- **Altar of Fangs** (Midnight) — a new three-boss dungeon that entered the pool when Season 2 began
- **Murder Row** (Midnight)
- **Den of Nalorakk** (Midnight)
- **The Blinding Vale** (Midnight)
- **Voidscar Arena** (Midnight)
- **Ruby Life Pools** (Dragonflight)
- **Kings’ Rest** (Battle for Azeroth)
- **Temple of Sethraliss** (Battle for Azeroth)

The Curse of Ula’tek notes describe the three returning dungeons as coming back with design and quality-of-life updates. Altar of Fangs was available up to Mythic 0 at the content update’s launch and joined Mythic+ a week later.

## Seasonal rewards Blizzard has confirmed

Official Season 2 Mythic+ rewards are tied to rating and end-of-season placement:

- **Midnight Keystone Conqueror: Season 2** — a Feat of Strength and the title “the Venomous,” at a Mythic+ rating of at least 1,500
- **Midnight Keystone Master: Season 2** — the Breath of Blight mount, at a rating of at least 2,000
- **Midnight Keystone Legend: Season 2** — the Breath of Ruin mount, at a rating of at least 3,000
- **Midnight Keystone Myth: Season 2** — listed with the title “the Venomous Contender.” The 12.1.5 content update, now live, set this achievement’s requirement at a Mythic+ rating of 3,600
- **Venomous Champion: Midnight Season 2** — for finishing the season in the top 1% of Mythic+ rating in your region
- **Venomous Hero: Midnight Season 2** — the title “the Venomous Hero,” for finishing in the top 0.1%

Timed runs at Mythic 10 or higher also unlock a dungeon portal for each of the eight dungeons.

## What official notes do not list

Blizzard’s Season 2 launch article does not publish the weekly Mythic+ affix schedule, keystone breakpoints, or affix tooltips. Community guides describe a ladder that includes Lindormi’s Guidance and Xal’atath’s Bargain, but those details are not in the official Season 2 or Curse of Ula’tek posts cited here, so they are omitted. Check the in-game keystone and Group Finder for the affixes actually active on your key this week.

The mid-season 12.1.5 update also returned Keystone Myth and said Blizzard would apply targeted dungeon tuning for difficulty outliers. It did not replace the eight-dungeon pool.

## What this means for your UI / MagguuUI

The Season 2 pool and portals are first-party systems. MagguuUI does not need a special module for the rotation to appear. After you time a +10, the official dungeon teleport is enough; you do not need an addon to add those portals. If you use a keystone or dungeon-timer plugin, confirm it still recognizes the current eight names, especially Altar of Fangs. Leave affix icons and routes to whatever the game and your plugins actually show this week, rather than to an unofficial schedule.
