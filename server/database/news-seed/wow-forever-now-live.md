World of Warcraft: Forever went live worldwide on November 4, 2026, at 3:00 p.m. PST (midnight, November 5, in Europe/Berlin). Blizzard positions it as a permanent game alongside modern World of Warcraft and existing Classic versions. Access is included with an active World of Warcraft subscription or Game Time.

The setting sits after *Warcraft III Reforged: Forsaken Kingdom* and before Molten Core. Players level to 60 across the original continents, then continue through new stories and ongoing updates.

## New ground in a familiar world

Eastern Kingdoms and Kalimdor are expanded with new and reimagined zones and more than 1,000 new quests from levels 1 to 60. Official destinations include:

- **Mount Hyjal**, set after Archimonde’s defeat, including Darkwhisper Gorge.
- **Shen’dralas**, a previously hidden area between Mulgore and Desolace, tied to the Shen’dralar, Eldre’Thalas, Dire Maul, and nearby centaur tribes.
- **The Riverglades**, a mid-30s to mid-40s frontier with more than 150 new quests.
- **Zephras Isle**, a level 1–12 starting experience with Classic-style presentation and architecture inspired by Skywall.

## Skyborne and new class combinations

The Skyborne — also called the shen’dorei, or “hidden people” — are a new playable race. Their story begins on Zephras Isle. At character creation you choose a faction: Horde-aligned Windshapers can become Shaman, and Alliance-aligned High-Order Skyborne can become Mages.

Forever also opens class combinations that were not available in original World of Warcraft, including Orc Mages, Troll Warlocks, Undead Paladins, Human Hunters, Gnome Priests, and Dwarf Shamans.

## Presets, rulesets, and names

The first time you log in, Forever asks you to choose an Experience Preset before you pick a Ruleset. **Classic** starts with standard-definition models, quest helpers off, one-bag and transmog off, camera-turning binds, and a Classic screen UI layout. **Enhanced** starts with high-definition models, those helpers on, one-bag and transmog on, strafe binds, and a centralized UI layout. Blizzard recommends Enhanced if you are unsure. The preset is only a starting point; options can be changed later under Game Menu > Options, and the preset screen does not return after the first login. Model definition can also be chosen per character and changed later in Options or at a Barbershop.

Instead of a traditional realm list, you choose a Ruleset: Normal, PvP, Roleplay, or Roleplay PvP. Each is a broader player ecosystem rather than a single realm. A Hardcore ruleset is planned for sometime after launch; no date has been published. Character names use a two-part structure so a full name is unique within a region.

## Systems around the journey

A camping system turns the Cooking profession’s Basic Campfire into a shared campsite. Nearby players can add up to three profession-crafted Camp Features (one each). Sitting or crafting there for at least one minute grants those benefits, which are mutually exclusive with the corresponding class buffs.

The Legacy System is an account-wide horizontal track with Challenges, Perks, and Rewards. Leveling alts is encouraged but not required; advantages can be unlocked on a single character. Forever also ships gamepad support for targeting, casting, looting, and menus.

## What this means for your UI / MagguuUI

MagguuUI maintains a separate Forever flavor, kept distinct from the Retail (Midnight) pack. Choose the Classic or Enhanced preset first so the default Blizzard layout matches how you want the built-in UI to behave, then load MagguuUI. Those options remain under Game Menu > Options after the preset screen is gone. Gamepad support is first-party; a desktop addon pack will not replace it, so gamepad users should confirm targeting, menus, and bags still work with their layout enabled.
